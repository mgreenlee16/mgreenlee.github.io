## Where I'm from

[Two or three sentences. Where you grew up, what it was like, anything about
it that shaped you.]

I was born in Waco, Texas in 2006 and mostly grew up in South Bend, IN. I have combined these experiences with my dad's ideas to make me a fan of the Dallas Cowboys and Notre Dame. 

## What I do when I'm not doing engineering

When I am not doing engineering, I enjoy being active. I spend most mornings in the gym, whether by myself or with friends, before the day actually starts. I played soccer growing up, captaining my high school team, and I enjoy both playing and watching every sport.

I am also a big fan of the culinary industry. Growing up, my brother and I taught ourselves to cook, and have expanded our skills as we have grown up. In high school, I worked for a local mexican grill as a chef, which I thoroughly enjoyed. Together, my brother and I have made everything, from smoked brisket to purely homemade nachos with fresh queso and guacamole.

## Fun Fact

One fun fact about me is that, despite my drastic fear of heights, I love skydiving. I am terrified of looking out the window when I am above the third story, yet I am fond of free-fall from 15,000 feet.
